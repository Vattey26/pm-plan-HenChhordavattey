# Stakeholders: first analysis

Two stakeholders have high influence and high interest: the **Head of the IT department (S01)** and the **supervising lecturer / PM (S02)**. The sponsor funds the project, approves the charter and decides at every phase gate, and expects an MVP demo in week 7 and a pilot in week 14. The PM carries the day-to-day responsibility and needs the sponsor's quick decisions on change requests, so these two get the most frequent contact: a weekly status report and a short check-in.

The **data protection officer / registrar (S09)** is the veto stakeholder: the pilot cannot run without approval to store student names and results, so the one-page data description must reach them well before week 13.
